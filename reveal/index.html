Advanced tracking techniques

Websites can secretly draw an image or process a sound. Tiny differences in your graphics card, drivers and system make the result slightly different on every computer — invisible to you, but easy to measure.

Installed fonts 0

By measuring the width of text, a site can detect which fonts you have installed. Fonts from Microsoft Office, Adobe or design apps also reveal which software you use.

How to protect yourself

  • Use a browser with fingerprinting protection. Brave, Firefox (Enhanced Tracking Protection → Strict) and Safari add noise or hide details. Tor Browser makes everyone look the same.
  • Blend in instead of standing out. Rare settings, unusual fonts and many extensions make you more unique, not less.
  • Private windows don't help here. They delete cookies and history, but your fingerprint stays the same.
  • Use a tracker blocker such as uBlock Origin — it stops many fingerprinting scripts from loading at all.
  • Remember your IP address. Every site sees it too (it's not shown here because this page contacts no server). A VPN hides it — but not your fingerprint.
